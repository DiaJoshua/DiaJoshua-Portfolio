From me, to you

My Lovey,

Some pages deserve a rewrite — not because the old one was wrong, but because we keep outgrowing every version of us I try to put into words.

Since the last page I built you, we crossed streets in another country together. We chased iced coffee and bánh mì through Saigon, got lost and called it exploring, and laughed at things no one else would ever understand. Watching you move through the world — camera in hand, that dress, that smile — I kept thinking the same thing: there is no one I would rather get lost with.

Thank you for being my calm and my chaos, my favorite hello and my hardest goodbye. For loving me on the bright days and holding me through the heavy ones. For making ordinary afternoons feel like places worth traveling to.

Whatever comes next — new cities, new songs, new versions of this page — I will keep choosing you. Every time.

I love you. Deeply, truly, always.

— Joshua 💌
