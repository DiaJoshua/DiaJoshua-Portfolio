# Joshua's Career Roadmap: ₱25k → ₱50k+ (July 2026 – end of 2027)

**Starting point:** Technical Support Engineer @ Trend Micro, ₱25,000/mo. BS CS (Digital Forensics), DevOps + full-stack background, NEP graduate, closed-case handling experience.

**Target:** SOC Analyst or security-focused engineer role, with networking + cloud foundations.

## Why SOC/Security is your best path

Your three assets stack perfectly for it: a Digital Forensics degree, daily hands-on work with enterprise security products at a known cybersecurity vendor, and log analysis/case-handling experience — that is literally the L1 SOC job description. Realistic Metro Manila pay (2026 data):

| Role | Entry (0–2 yrs) | With ~2 yrs + certs |
|---|---|---|
| SOC Analyst L1 | ₱30k–50k/mo | ₱50k–80k+/mo |
| Technical Support Engineer L2 (other vendors) | ₱35k–60k/mo | ₱60k+/mo |
| Cloud / Network Engineer | ₱40k–57k/mo avg | ₱60k–100k+/mo |

Sources vary widely (Glassdoor junior SOC figures are lower; SalaryExpert/Indeed averages higher) — treat these as ranges, and note night-shift SOC roles often add 10–20% differential.

## Phase 0 — UDEMY SPRINT (now → Aug 31, 2026) ⏰

Your paid Udemy dies Aug 31. Priorities in strict order — finishing #1 matters more than starting all four:

1. **CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) full course** — Jason Dion or Andrew Ramdayal (~30h). This is the backbone; the exam is your single highest-ROI cert.
2. **Networking fundamentals / CCNA-level basics** — Neil Anderson's CCNA course, at least through OSI model, TCP/IP, subnetting, common ports/protocols (~15–20h of it). SOC interviews always test this.
3. **Linux essentials** (~10h) — navigation, permissions, logs, grep.
4. **If time remains:** AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals) short course (~8h).

Pace: 2h/weekday + 4h per weekend day ≈ 65h over 5 weeks — enough for #1 fully and most of #2.

**Do immediately, before access ends:**
- Download/screenshot every Udemy completion certificate as you finish — they vanish with the account.
- Check your email for an **ISC2 CC exam code** — if you enrolled in the free "1M Certified in Cybersecurity" program before it closed May 20, 2026, your code is valid until Dec 31, 2026. Free cert if you have it.
- Use **Trend Campus / internal training** while employed: Vision One and Apex One certification courses cost outsiders real money through partners — earn whatever internal product certification you can for free. "Trend Micro Vision One Certified" on a resume is gold for SOC applications.

## Phase 1 — CERTIFY (Sep – Nov 2026)

| Cert | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Microsoft SC-900** (Security Fundamentals) | **FREE** | Attend a Microsoft Virtual Training Day (events.microsoft.com), get exam voucher (~90-day validity) |
| **Microsoft AZ-900** (Azure Fundamentals) | **FREE** | Same voucher route — covers your cloud checkbox |
| **Fortinet FCF / FCA** | **FREE** | Free self-paced training + exam at training.fortinet.com — second security vendor on your resume |
| **CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)** | ~US$400 (~₱23k) | **The one worth paying for.** It's the HR filter for SOC jobs. Take it ~Oct after the Udemy course + practice exams (Dion's practice tests). Yes it hurts at ₱25k salary — it's the highest-ROI ₱23k you'll spend. |
| ISC2 CC | US$199 | Only if you have the free code (see Phase 0); otherwise skip — Security+ outranks it |

**Practice (parallel, ongoing):** TryHackMe **SOC Level 1 path** (~US$14/mo premium, several rooms free) — SIEM (Splunk/ELK), Wireshark, phishing analysis, Windows/Linux logs, MITRE ATT&CK. Cisco **Skills for All** (free) for networking practice. These give you concrete things to say in interviews.

**Visibility:** Create/clean LinkedIn (the resume has a placeholder for it), pin your ClickSmart project on GitHub, write 2–3 short investigation write-ups (e.g., "Analyzing a phishing email header", "Detecting X in Windows Event Logs") on LinkedIn/Medium.

## Phase 2 — APPLY (Oct 2026 onward, overlapping Phase 1)

Don't wait for all certs — start applying once Security+ is *scheduled*. "Security+ (exam scheduled Oct 2026)" is a legitimate resume line.

- **Where:** MSSPs and SOCs (Accenture, IBM, Infosys BPM, Trustwave, banks — BDO/UnionBank/Metrobank, fintech — GCash/Maya, telcos — Globe/PLDT); security-focused TSE at other vendors; NOC analyst as a bridge if needed.
- **Job boards:** JobStreet, LinkedIn, Indeed PH, Kalibrr + direct careers pages. Referrals beat portals — mine Trend colleagues who moved on.
- **Negotiation:** with Trend Micro on your resume + Security+ + lab practice, ask ₱40–45k, accept from ₱35k. That's a 40–80% jump. Below ₱32k, decline unless the learning/brand is exceptional.
- **Keep your Trend job until an offer is signed.**

## Phase 3 — DEEPEN (2027)

After 6–12 months in a SOC/security role, pick one:
- **Blue-team depth:** CompTIA CySA+ or Security Blue Team **BTL1** (£399) → SOC L2 / incident response
- **Network path:** CCNA (US$330) → network security engineer
- **Cloud security:** AZ-500 or SC-200 → cloud security ops (highest ceiling)

Target by late 2027 / 2028: **₱60k–90k/mo.**

## Interview prep checklist (start now, 15 min/day)

CIA triad · TCP/IP & common ports · TCP vs UDP · what happens when you type a URL · phishing indicators · malware types · SIEM vs EDR vs XDR (you support an XDR — use it!) · incident response lifecycle · Windows Event IDs (4624/4625/4688) · how you handle an irate customer (your TSE stories are perfect STAR material)
